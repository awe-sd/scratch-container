  • No pad, laydown yard, cranes, foundations, or road access have appeared at the IA POI as of 2026-07-01
  • The queue-reported COD 2028-01-01 does not match the signed CenterPoint SGIA: Exhibit B Schedule gives Scheduled COD = later of 2029-01-12 or 3 months after TIF In-Service (which is itself later of 2028-10-12 or 36 months after Prerequisite Items).   • Physical feasibility for 2028-01-01: ruled out. GE 7HA turbines have ~24-36 month OEM lead time; the TEF response 2025-04-16 states turbines were still being negotiated (sources/2025-04-16_puct_56896-73_emberyork-response-to-denial.pdf); no public GE order announcement retrieved. From bare land at 2026-07-01, plant commissioning by 2028-01 is not physically achievable for a 900 MW simple-cycle site (multi-year build).
  • Financing overhang: Texas Energy Fund loan HARD-DENIED for due-diligence failure, PUCT Docket 56896 order 2025-04-15 explicitly 'not subject to motions for rehearing or appeal' (sources/2025-04-15_puct_56896-71_order-denying-loan-app.pdf). EYEC's stated substitute (First-round equity commitment from an unnamed A-credit-parent asset owner + Paul Hastings 'Term Sheet C') was a proposal at April 2025 and no closed-facility announcement located since (sources/2025-04-16_puct_56896-73_emberyork-response-to-denial.pdf).
