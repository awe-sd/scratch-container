## 7. COD assessment

- Contractual grounding: signed SGIA Scheduled COD is **2029-01-12**, a full year AFTER the queue-reported 2028-01-01 — the queue number is stale/misaligned with the executed agreement; ERCOT queue does not yet reflect the IA amendment provisions
- Physical feasibility for 2028-01-01: **rule-out**. Simple-cycle GE 7HA plant construction ≈ 24-30 months from NTP + turbines have ~24-36 month OEM lead time. TEF response admits turbines were still being negotiated (not ordered) at April 2025; no public GE order announcement located; site is bare mid-2026. First fire before ~2029 is not possible.
- Financing overhang: TEF loan hard-denied 2025-04-15, no cure path; EYEC's stated alternative (private A-credit equity + Paul Hastings term sheet C) remained a *proposal* as of April 2025 — no closed-facility announcement retrieved. The **$99.157M LC due 2026-12-12** is a hard-money test — failure to post terminates the IA under Section 2.1.
- Cross-signal: sister project EmberGreen (twin 900 MW) tells the Comptroller its own build is **2026-2029**, incentive from **2030** ([J0028 rec packet Attachment A](sources/2026-04-15_comptroller_J0028_embergreen-wharton-jeti-recpkt.pdf)) — that is EmberClear's own internal timeline, consistent with EmberYork IA 2029-Q1 COD, not queue 2028-Q1
- **Independent estimate: 2030-Q2, drift risk high** — best case is the IA schedule (2029-Q1) held; but with land unbought, turbines unordered, financing unclosed, and LC unposted 5 months before its 2026-12-12 deadline, the near path is a slip of at least 12-18 months from the IA date
