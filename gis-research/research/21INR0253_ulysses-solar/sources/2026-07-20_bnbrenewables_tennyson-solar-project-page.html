BNB TENNYSON Solar

Located on the eastern edge of Coke County, BNB Tennyson Solar is a 150MWac project that taps into a double-circuited 345kV CREZ line. The project will be built on land covering over 1200 acres and will benefit the community at large with various “outreach” agreements. BNB sold BNB Tennyson Solar to Akuo Energy USA in 2021 to advance the project through to construction, which is expected to commence in early 2022, with an expected COD in 2023.

Project Overview:

  • Location: Coke County, Texas

  • Size: 150MWac

  • Status: Under Development (Projected COD: 2023)
