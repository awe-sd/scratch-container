# TotalEnergies Capital Recycling PR — Dec 4, 2024

URL: https://corporate.totalenergies.us/news/totalenergies-implements-its-strategy-capital-recycling-acquisition-germany-and-farm-down-us

## Summary
TotalEnergies farmed down 50% of a ~2 GW US solar+BESS portfolio to Apollo ($800M):
- Danish Fields solar
- Cottonwood solar
- Hill Solar I
- Two battery storage projects (300 MW total)

**Myrtle Solar is NOT listed** as part of this farm-down. The image filename in the PR shows "Myrtle Solar Farm (Texas, USA)" as a file reference, but the project is not in the portfolio sold.

TotalEnergies retains 50% + remains operator of all sold assets.
