Leyline Renewable Capital Provides Growth Capital to Launch New Energy Storage Developer, Navitas Energy

DURHAM, N.C., Oct. 6, 2022

Leyline Renewable Capital announced a partnership with Navitas Energy, providing early-stage growth capital to develop 500–1000 MW of standalone storage in the ERCOT market over five years.

Leyline offers non-dilutive financing, preserving founder control while enabling portfolio growth. The firm also brings over 30 years of project development experience as on-call support for the Houston-based Navitas.

CEO Erik Lensch stated: "Navitas' founder has a wealth of experience in the development, optimization, and trading of power generation assets."

Vipul Devluk, director of originations, noted: "Bringing more energy storage to ERCOT is critical to increasing the capacity and deployment of renewable energy."

About Leyline Renewable Capital

Invests in development, construction, and financing of renewable energy projects, bridging the gap between development and construction phases.

About Navitas Energy

Texas-based developer of energy storage and flexible load, with 28 years of experience across thousands of MWs of power generation assets.
