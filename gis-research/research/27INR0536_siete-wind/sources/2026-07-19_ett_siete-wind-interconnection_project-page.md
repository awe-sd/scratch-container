# Source: ETT Siete Wind Interconnection Project Page
## URL: https://www.ettexas.com/Projects/SieteWindInterconnection
## Fetched: 2026-07-19
## Relevance: Confirms developer, location, POI, and interconnection details

## Full text:
"Siete Wind, LLC. has requested to interconnect a 375 MW wind generating facility called "Siete Wind" in Webb County, TX. The interconnection will be located along the ETT Lobo to Avanzada 345kV line at a new 345kV station ("Triada") owned by ETT. ETT would construct a new three (3) Breaker Ring bus at the Triada 345 kV Station, along the Lobo to Fowlerton 345 kV line."

## Key facts:
- Developer SPV: Siete Wind, LLC
- Capacity: 375 MW (ETT's request; queue entry lists 492.8 MW — capacity difference)
- County: Webb County, TX
- POI: New 345kV station named Triada owned by ETT
- Line tapped: ETT Lobo to Avanzada 345kV line / Lobo to Fowlerton 345kV line
- Status: In Development (as of 2026-07-19)
