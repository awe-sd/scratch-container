FGE Power Announces Sale of FGE Goodnight Wind Farm Project to Omega Energia

AUSTIN, Texas, July 18, 2022 — FGE Power, LLC, Austin-based developers of large-scale renewable energy projects, announced the sale of 100% ownership of the FGE Goodnight wind project to Omega Energia, a renewable energy company headquartered in São Paulo, Brazil.

The transaction enabled simultaneous construction commencement on FGE Goodnight I (265.5MW) and near-term prospects for FGE Goodnight II (also 265.5MW), totaling an aggregate capacity of 531MW. Both phases are targeted for commercial operations by end of 2023. Vestas' V136-4.5MW turbines were selected for Phase I, with IEA as EPC provider.

FGE Goodnight III (200MW+) is in early development stages. Upon completion, the overall project is projected to be the largest wind farm in Texas and among the largest worldwide.

Emerson G. Farrell, Founder and Chairman of FGE Power, stated: "Texas needed more reliable, sustainable, and dependable sources of energy...even more so today with the impacts of climate change."

Antonio Bastos Filho, CEO of Omega Energia, noted: "FGE's team did an extraordinary job developing Goodnight combining key attributes that lead to a successful wind complex."
