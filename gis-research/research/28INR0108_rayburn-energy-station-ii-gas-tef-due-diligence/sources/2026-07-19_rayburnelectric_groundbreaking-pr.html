SHERMAN, Texas, June 10, 2026 — Rayburn Electric Cooperative yesterday broke ground on Rayburn Energy Station II (RES II), a $685 million, 570-megawatt natural gas generation facility that will strengthen reliability for more than 625,000 Texans served by Rayburn’s four Member cooperatives across North Texas.

The project is being financed in part through the Texas Energy Fund, a state initiative created following Winter Storm Uri. Rayburn was the only electric cooperative selected during the program’s initial phase, chosen from more than 125 applicants.

Located adjacent to Rayburn Energy Station I (RES I) in Sherman, RES II will provide firm, dispatchable generation during periods of peak demand, bolstering reliability across the ERCOT region.

“RES II is about ensuring the communities we serve have reliable power for decades to come,” said David A. Naylor, President and CEO of Rayburn Electric Cooperative. “After Winter Storm Uri, we made a commitment to strengthen reliability by investing in critical infrastructure and greater control over our energy future. That commitment is what you see taking shape today.”

The June 9 groundbreaking ceremony brought together Texas energy, regulatory, and community leaders to celebrate the start of construction. Speakers included David A. Naylor, President and CEO of Rayburn Electric Cooperative; Mayor Shawn Teamann of the City of Sherman; Representative Katrina Pierson; and Commissioner Kathleen Jackson of the Public Utility Commission of Texas.

“Reliable power is one of the most important factors driving economic growth in North Texas,” said Mayor Shawn Teamann. “Rayburn Energy Station II strengthens Sherman’s position as a destination for investment, job creation, and long-term development while ensuring the electric infrastructure is in a place to support future growth.”

Rayburn selected Siemens Energy and Primoris Services Corporation as key partners in the development of RES II.

Siemens Energy will supply ten SGT-800 industrial gas turbines, providing the plant’s core generation technology. The SGT-800 is a proven, high-efficiency turbine platform designed for fast-start peaking and intermediate-cycle operation, making it well-suited to ERCOT’s real-time dispatch environment and the reliability demands of North Texas.

“With ten of our SGT-800 gas turbines onsite, Rayburn Energy Station II will have a total capacity of 570 MW and the ability to deliver reliable, fast-start power to support their members during periods of peak demand, strengthening grid resilience across Texas as energy needs continue to grow,” said David Blank, Sales Director for North America at Siemens Energy.

Primoris Services Corporation will serve as the engineering, procurement, and construction partner for the project, bringing the infrastructure and execution discipline required to deliver a generation facility of this scale and complexity on a defined timeline.

“The groundbreaking of Rayburn Energy Station II Facility represents an important milestone for energy resiliency in Texas. We’re honored to partner with Rayburn Electric Cooperative to safely and efficiently bring new generation capacity online, strengthening system reliability and supporting our communities for decades to come,” said Heath Moncrief, President – Energy Segment, Primoris Services Corporation.

The project is expected to create approximately 250 construction jobs and 14 permanent operational positions, with commercial operations planned for summer 2028. When complete, RES I and RES II will form a combined generation complex exceeding 1,300 megawatts in one of the fastest-growing regions in Texas.

About Rayburn Electric Cooperative

Rayburn Electric Cooperative, headquartered in Rockwall, Texas, is a not-for-profit generation and transmission cooperative providing safe, reliable, and affordable power to four Member-owned distribution cooperatives — Fannin Electric Cooperative, Farmers Electric Cooperative, Grayson-Collin Electric Cooperative, and Trinity Valley Electric Cooperative — collectively serving more than 625,000 Texans. For more information, visit www.rayburnelectric.com.

About Primoris Services Corporation

Primoris Services Corporation is a leading provider of critical infrastructure services to the utility, energy, and renewables markets throughout the United States and Canada. We deliver a range of engineering, construction, and maintenance capabilities that power, connect, and enhance society. On projects spanning utility-scale solar, renewables, power delivery, communications, power generation, and transportation infrastructure, we offer unmatched value to our clients, a safe and entrepreneurial culture to our employees, and innovation and excellence to our communities. To learn more, visit https://www.prim.com/ and follow us on social media @PrimorisServicesCorporation.

About the Texas Energy Fund

The Texas Energy Fund was created by the Texas Legislature and ratified by voters in November 2023 to finance the development of new, dispatchable power generation capacity within the ERCOT grid. The program is administered by the Public Utility Commission of Texas.
