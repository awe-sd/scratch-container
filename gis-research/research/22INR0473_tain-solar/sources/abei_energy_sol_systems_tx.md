# Source: Sol Systems acquires 190 MW Texas solar project from ABEI Energy

- URL: https://solsystems.com/news/sol-systems-acquires-190-mw-texas-solar-project-from-abei-energy/
- Also covered: https://renewablesnow.com/news/sol-systems-buys-190-mw-texas-solar-project-in-texas-811601/

## Key facts
- ABEI Energy is a Madrid-based independent power producer with >15 GW under development worldwide
- Sold a 190.3 MWp Texas solar project (Uvalde County) to Sol Systems
- ABEI continued to support pre-construction development after the sale
- ABEI uses "Green VII LLC" and similar subsidiaries for individual project SPVs
- TAIN SOLAR (22INR0473) is listed as developer: "Abei Energy Green VII LLC"

## Relevance to TAIN SOLAR
- Developer identity confirmed: ABEI Energy (Spain), SPV = Abei Energy Green VII LLC
- ABEI has a pattern of developing-then-selling Texas solar projects
- Tain Solar may follow the same develop-and-sell pattern
