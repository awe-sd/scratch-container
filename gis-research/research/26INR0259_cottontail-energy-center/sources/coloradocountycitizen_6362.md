# Source: Colorado County Citizen — "County mulls tax break for battery facility"
URL: https://coloradocountycitizen.com/article/6362
Retrieved: 2026-07-18

Developer: Mission Clean Energy
Project: Cottontail Energy Center (200.85 MW lithium-ion battery storage)
Date: June 23, 2025 — Commissioners Court vote; 3-2 approved reinvestment zone designation
Site: 18.3-acre property NW of Hwy 71 / I-10 intersection, near Glidden, Colorado County TX
Abatement: 100% tax abatement requested; partial abatement or PILOTs also discussed
Projected groundbreaking: 2027; 18-month construction; ~5 years to full operation
Tax revenue estimate: $6.4M over 10 years (full abatement scenario)
Jobs: 83 construction, 16 operational
Note: Commissioners designated reinvestment zone but none spoke in favor of abatement itself
