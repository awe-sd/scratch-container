# Source: Colorado County Citizen — "County denies tax break for battery facility"
URL: https://coloradocountycitizen.com/article/6532
Retrieved: 2026-07-18

Developer: Mission Clean Energy
Project: Cottontail Energy Center
Key dates: May 22 (abatement requested), June 23 (reinvestment zone approved), July 14 2025 (final vote: DENIED)
Site: 18.3-acre property NW of Hwy 71 / I-10, near residential homes, Glidden Baptist Church, CO County EMS, Precinct 1 Constable
Denial reasons: safety/location (proximity to residents + emergency services), insufficient community outreach, limited long-term economic benefit, short construction window
Battery chemistry: lithium iron phosphate (LFP — lower thermal risk)
Design: outdoor facility, no enclosing structure
LCRA controls electricity distribution
Mission Clean Energy plans to SELL the development post-construction (will not operate)
Commissioners acknowledged they lack authority to block construction outright
