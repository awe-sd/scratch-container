Key evidence

  • Sentinel-2 wide-frame chip (imagery/s2_2026-07-10_xwide.png) reveals a ~3,000-acre graded/cleared polygon matching the exact shape of Cypress Creek's own Ch313 Improvements Map boundary
  • Monthly timelapse (imagery/s2_2024-07-01.png through s2_2026-07-01.png, plus dekad frames to 2026-07-11): site undisturbed through 2025-03-01; a bright graded substation pad appears exactly at the mapped substation location by 2025-04-01 (tight bracket, absent 03-01/present 04-01)
  • Grading/clearing expands to ~2/3 of the polygon by 2025-06-01 and to essentially the full footprint by 2025-09-01
  • Darker, more uniform rectangular patches consistent with possible racking/panel installation appear from 2026-03-01 onward, but 10m Sentinel-2 resolution cannot confirm this over bare graded soil - no unambiguous full-field panel signature observed through 2026-07-11
  • The reported COD (2027-04-17) is not merely a developer claim: it is the exact Scheduled Commercial Operation Date in Amendment No. 1 to the signed Oncor-Hanson Solar interconnection agreement (PUCT Control No. 35077, filed 2024-08-15) - this confirms the date is contractually grounded, but because the queue's projectCod and the IA schedule likely share a common source, this is NOT independent evidence the date is achievable, only that it is real and not fabricated
  • The schedule already slipped once, by about 18 months, between the original IA (2023-09-20, Scheduled COD 2025-10-21) and Amendment No. 1 (2024, Scheduled COD 2027-04-17) - this occurred before construction began, showing the project is not immune to schedule risk
  • Independent imagery evidence shows construction progressing broadly in step with the amended schedule: site-wide grading essentially complete by ~2025-09, stable extensive graded footprint through mid-2026, with the contractual In-Service Date (2026-12-03) about 5 months out from the latest clear imagery (2026-07-11)
