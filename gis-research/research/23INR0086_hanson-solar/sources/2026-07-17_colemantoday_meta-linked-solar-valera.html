Flood Watch until FRI 7:00 PM CDT

Weather Alert

...FLOOD WATCH REMAINS IN EFFECT UNTIL 7 PM CDT THIS EVENING...

* WHAT...Flash flooding caused by excessive rainfall continues to be
possible.

* WHERE...A portion of west central Texas, including the following
counties, Brown, Coke, Coleman, Concho, Crockett, Fisher, Irion,
Kimble, Mason, McCulloch, Menard, Nolan, Runnels, San Saba,
Schleicher, Sterling, Sutton, Taylor and Tom Green.

* WHEN...Until 7 PM CDT this evening.

* IMPACTS...There is a potential for life threatening flooding within
the watch area. Considerable to locally catastrophic flash flooding
impacts are likely for portions of the Northern Edwards Plateau
including portions of Crockett and Sutton counties. Excessive
runoff may result in flooding of rivers, creeks, streams, and other
low-lying and flood-prone locations. Creeks and streams may rise
out of their banks. Flooding will occur in poor drainage and urban
areas. Low-water crossings will be flooded. Extensive street
flooding is likely and flooding of creeks and rivers is possible.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
- Multiple rounds of heavy rainfall are expected over portions of
West Central Texas through this evening. High moisture values
will fuel highly efficient rain-producing storms, with model
guidance indicating near-stationary clusters capable of producing
2 to 4 inches of rain per hour. This could lead to additional
rainfall totals today of 2 to 6 inches over the Watch area, with
isolated totals even higher.
- http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

You should monitor later forecasts and be prepared to take action
should Flash Flood Warnings be issued. Those living in areas prone to
flooding should be prepared to take action should flooding develop.

Meta-Linked Solar Development Now Underway in Valera, Completion Expected by 2027

A 396-megawatt solar energy project is coming to Coleman County, bringing clean power, local tax revenue, and hundreds of jobs to the rural community near Valera.

Hanson Solar, led by Cypress Creek Renewables (CCR) in partnership with TIC – The Industrial Company, is expected to break ground later this year. Once operational in 2027, the project will deliver enough electricity to power more than 75,000 Texas homes annually, while contributing to Meta’s goal of sourcing 100% renewable energy.

“This agreement with Meta enables us to move forward with confidence,” said Sarah Slusser, CEO of Cypress Creek Renewables, in the February announcement. “We’re proud to support Meta’s sustainability goals while investing in Coleman County’s economic future.” (Source: CCR press release, Feb. 26, 2025)

Spanning up to 3,000 acres near Valera, the solar farm will include over 1.24 million solar panels and 104 inverters, along with a large-scale battery energy storage system to improve grid stability. The project is expected to interconnect with the ERCOT power grid via a 345-kilovolt (kV) transmission line.

According to the Texas Comptroller’s Office, CCR is investing approximately $356.9 million into the project, which is being developed through Hanson Solar, LLC, a project-specific entity formed by Cypress Creek. The project secured a Chapter 313 value limitation agreement with Panther Creek CISD and will generate over $80 million in local tax revenue during its lifetime. 

“Hanson Solar will drive millions of dollars in economic benefits for our host community,” CCR stated in its release. “From the construction jobs to long-term tax base growth, we’re committed to ensuring Coleman County thrives alongside this project.”

CCR also emphasized its long-term responsibility for site stewardship, noting that the project was designed with the local landscape in mind.

“We strive to be good neighbors in every community we serve,” the company said. “That means responsible land use, collaboration with local officials, and transparent project updates from planning to decommissioning.”

The project will create an estimated 300+ construction jobs, and while ongoing maintenance staff needs are relatively low, landowners leasing to the project will receive steady income over the life of the solar farm. 

According to pv magazine USA, the partnership with Meta reflects a broader trend of major tech companies investing in utility-scale solar projects to meet internal sustainability benchmarks.

“Meta’s commitment helps unlock renewable energy projects like Hanson Solar,” Slusser added. “It’s a model for how corporate demand can drive real progress on the ground.” (Source: pv magazine USA, March 3, 2025)

Cypress Creek noted that construction is expected to begin before the end of 2025, with the solar farm generating power by 2027. Once online, Hanson Solar will contribute significantly to ERCOT’s renewable portfolio — while also positioning Coleman County as a key player in Texas’s clean energy transition.
