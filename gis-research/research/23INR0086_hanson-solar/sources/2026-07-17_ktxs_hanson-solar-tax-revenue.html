New solar project in Coleman County to produce over $80M in local tax revenue

by Felicity Neptune

Tue, August 5, 2025 at 11:27 AM
New solar project in Coleman County to produce over $80M in local tax revenue (Eloi_Omella/GettyImages)

    topics:

  • Solar project
  • Coleman County
  • Cypress Creek Renewables
  • Meta
  • Hanson Solar
  • Local tax revenue
  • ERCOT grid
  • Clean energy

COLEMAN COUNTY, Texas — A new solar facility is being built in Coleman County.

Cypress Creek Renewables and Meta have signed an agreement that will bring a 505MWdc solar facility, named Hanson Solar, to the area.

Hanson Solar is expected to deliver electricity to the ERCOT grid.

“We are thrilled to expand our partnership with Meta and provide them with the clean energy needed to support their data center operations and sustainability goals,” Sarah Slusser, CEO of Cypress Creek Renewables, stated in a press release. “Hanson Solar represents a transformative milestone for our company, strengthening our growth, enhancing energy independence, and driving millions of dollars in economic benefits for our host community.”

RELATED | 5,000 acre solar project in Stonewall County aims to boost power grid and local economy

Cypress Creek officials said the project is expected to provide over $80 million in local tax revenue and more than 300 jobs during its construction.

“We are thrilled to collaborate with Cypress Creek Renewables to bring solar energy to the grid in Texas – and support our operations in the region with clean and renewable energy,” said Urvi Parekh, Head of Global Energy at Meta. “We look forward to this project becoming operational.”

Construction for the facility is expected to begin this year with the facility to open in 2027.
