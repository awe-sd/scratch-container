The Texas Energy Fund

PLACEHOLDER H2 TO MEET WCAG REQUIREMENT

Overview

The Texas Energy Fund (TxEF) provides grants and loans to finance the construction, maintenance, and modernization of electric facilities in Texas. Since the TxEF's launch in 2024:

  • For the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) grid, the TxEF has allocated $3.65 billion for loans and $172.56 million for bonus grants to date to support 5,516 megawatts of new, reliable power generation to serve hundreds of thousands of homes and businesses.
  • For grids outside the ERCOT region, the TxEF has allocated $649.1 million for grants to date to weatherize facilities and strengthen electric reliability and resiliency for over 1,064,800 Texans.

Through a rigorous, competitive application and review process, TxEF loans and grants provide funding for electric infrastructure projects across four programs:

In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program
Completion Bonus Grant Program
Outside ERCOT Grant Program
Texas Backup Power Package Program

Ready to apply?

Access the TxEF Portal to upload your application materials and submit your application.

For detailed application guidelines and information about the programs mentioned above, please visit TxEF Online.

Have questions? Find answers on our FAQ page.

 

In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program

The In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program provides low-interest loans to qualifying companies for the construction of new or expansion of existing dispatchable electric generating facilities in the ERCOT power region. Qualifying projects must add at least 100 megawatts (MW) of new dispatchable generation capacity to the ERCOT grid. Loan amounts may not exceed 60% of total project costs.

PUCT rule 16 TAC §25.510 establishes program rules, eligibility requirements, loan terms, evaluation criteria, and the application process.

The PUCT evaluated applications based on the applicant's experience and strength of financing, as well as the proposed project's technical and financial attributes, location, speed to market, and generation resource type, among other factors.

The program provides 20-year loans at a fixed 3% interest rate. Amortization schedules vary with each agreement. This program is capped at financing no more than 10,000 MW of new generation.

Completion Bonus Grant Program

The Completion Bonus Grant (CBG) Program will provide grants to qualifying companies for projects that supply power to the ERCOT grid before June 1, 2029. Qualifying projects must add at least 100 MW of capacity to the ERCOT power region. To be eligible for a CBG, an electric generation resource must have met certain planning requirements after June 1, 2023.

PUCT rule 16 TAC §25.511 establishes the application process, eligibility criteria, and performance requirements for the CBG Program.

CBG awards are paid over a 10-year period. Annual payments are contingent upon the performance of the power plant. Performance is measured by ERCOT during an annual "test period" and compared to the performance of a reference group of other generation resources in the ERCOT region.

Outside ERCOT Grant Program

The Outside ERCOT Grant Program (OEGP) provides existing transmission and distribution infrastructure and electric generating facilities grants for:

  • facility modernization
  • facility weatherization
  • reliability and resiliency
  • vegetation management

PUCT rule 16 TAC §25.512 establishes a rigorous application and review process for OEGP grants, including eligibility requirements, and grant award and payment process information.

As of December 18, 2025, the PUCT has selected 29 eligible OEGP projects. The PUCT and the applicants of those projects are working to reach grant agreements for each project.

The projects total $964.5 million and will strengthen facility weatherization and electric reliability and resiliency for over 1.5 million Texans served by grids outside the ERCOT region.

Texas Backup Power Package Program

The Texas Backup Power Package (TBPP) Program will provide funding for qualifying entities to design, procure, and install backup power equipment at facilities that support community health, safety, and well-being — like hospitals and nursing homes. A backup power package is a stand-alone, multiday backup power source that provides power directly to a facility without passing through a utility electric meter.

On January 14, 2024, the PUCT named members of the Texas Backup Power Package Advisory Committee, which recommended criteria to the PUCT for backup power package funding.

PUCT rule 16 TAC §25.513 establishes eligibility criteria, application requirements, the evaluation process, grant and loan terms, and compliance obligations.

The PUCT will host a workshop on July 20, 2026, at 1:00 p.m. central time to solicit input regarding procurement of vendors for the TBPP Program. The workshop is open to the public and will be held in the Commissioners' Hearing Room at 1701 N. Congress Ave., Austin, Texas 78701 (7th Floor). It will also be streamed on the PUCT Broadcasts webpage. Agenda and discussion questions are posted under Project No. 59939. Written comments may be filed under Project No. 59939.

TEXAS ENERGY FUND ADVISORY COMMITTEE

The Texas Energy Fund Advisory Committee was established by Senate Bill 2627 to provide comments and recommendations to the Commission on the development and adoption of TxEF rules. The Committee will review the overall operation, function, and structure of the fund at least semiannually.

Members of the TxEF Advisory Committee include:

  • Senator Charles Schwertner, Co-Chair
  • Senator Joan Huffman
  • Senator Nathan Johnson
  • Representative Caroline Fairly
  • Representative Todd Hunter
  • Representative Ana Hernandez

TEXAS ENERGY FUND BACKGROUND

The TxEF was created by the 88th Texas Legislature through Senate Bill 2627, known as the Powering Texas Forward Act, and Texans approved the TxEF through a constitutional election on November 7, 2023. The Legislature appropriated $5 billion to fund the TxEF for Fiscal Years 2024-2025 and an additional $4 billion for Fiscal Years 2026-2027.
