# Source: Boerne Star coverage — Ringtail Ridge Storage

URLs:
- https://www.boernestar.com/article/7151 (250+ residents oppose, ~Jan 2024)
- https://www.boernestar.com/article/9257 (county moves against abatement/TCEQ, ~Jan 2024)

Key facts:
- Developer: Key Capture Energy; LLC entity: KCE TX 26, LLC; contact: Erica Glenn (developer)
- Site: Flat Rock Creek Road off FM 473, near LCRA substation in Comfort, Kendall County TX; 3 acres of 8-acre leased plat
- Technology: Lithium-ion BESS; 20-year lease term; ~$80M build cost
- POI: LCRA substation (matches GIS queue "7150 Kendall 138kV")
- Community opposition: 250-275+ residents at Jan 2024 public meeting; 30+ speakers (fire safety, water table, emergency response)
- Precinct 4 Commissioner pledged no abatement
- County Commissioners passed resolution requesting TCEQ deny all BESS facility permits in county
- Commissioners moved to form Chapter 391 Sub-Regional Planning Commission (with Kerr County) to coordinate BESS oversight at state level
- No abatement granted; tax abatement application pending/denied
- No construction timeline stated; still pre-construction as of Jan 2024
