Parcel / boundary maps from filings — basis for the site fix

Derivation: Ch.313 agreement #1728 (Graford ISD) Checklist Item #11 includes actual MAP exhibits (highest provenance rung): a project-boundary/solar-panel-footprint map and a 'Proposed Reinvestment Zone' map anchored at a labeled landmark, 'Marluc Bella Vita Ranch'. That ranch/lodge's own site (lakegodstone.com, WebFetch) gives its address as '4636 Halsell Ranch Road, Graford, TX 76449' -- independently matching the executed IA's Exhibit C interconnection-details text, which names the POI 'Halsell Ranch Switch' on the Thomas Price-Willow Creek 345kV line in Jack County (the station-location sentence itself is REDACTED as CEII per Oncor's PUCT cover letter). Cross-referenced the place name via Overpass/OSM: 'Halsell Ranch Road' (TIGER, tiger:county=Jack,TX, spanning 33.030-33.046N/-98.331 to -98.271W) and 'Halsell Ranch Cemetery' (GNIS feature_id 1337264, a federally-documented named place) at 33.0318N,-98.2947W -- used as the anchor. Three independent public records (Ch.313 map, IA Exhibit C place name, GNIS/TIGER) converge; no single rung was sufficient alone (IA coordinates redacted; Ch.313 map has no lat/lon graticule). NOT parcel-precise -- a ~1km-radius place-name anchor. Nominatim and the Census geocoder both returned zero matches for the literal street address (too rural); Overpass/TIGER was the path that worked. Full chain: sources/SITE_DERIVATION.md.
