# Source: Double Mountain Chronicle — Fisher County solar / hospital article

URL: https://www.doublemountainchronicle.com/news/fisher-county-hospital-expects-21-million-boost-solar-energy-development-moves-forward

## Key facts
- Developer: Innovative Solar Solutions (ISS); project designation IS245
- Location: southeastern Fisher County, Texas; connected to Lone Star transmission line
- Part of a 690 MW portfolio across four Texas projects
- Fisher County Commissioners approved a tax abatement (Dec prior to article); ~50% effective rate
- Fisher County Hospital District approved $217k/year over 10-year abatement term ($2.1M total)
- Construction anticipated to begin Q2 2025 (per article date)
- Representatives named: David Ramm and Derrick Peters (ISS)
