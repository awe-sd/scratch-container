# Source: Renewable Mirror — Sonoco + ENGIE VPPA delivery
URL: https://renewablemirror.com/sonoco-and-engie-begin-vppa-delivery-from-big-sampson-wind-project-in-texas/
Fetched: 2026-07-18

## Key facts
- Developer/Owner: ENGIE North America
- Capacity: ~270 MW (60 turbines × 4.5 MW)
- Location: Crockett County, Texas
- COD: Late 2025 (VPPA delivery commenced)
- Offtaker: Sonoco Products Company, 15-year VPPA, ~140 MW/year
- Construction: ~400 workers during build; 10–15 permanent ops staff
- Economic: >$60M projected tax revenues to Crockett County + local school districts
- VPPA covers ~52% of Big Sampson's expected output; ~83% of Sonoco's US electricity
