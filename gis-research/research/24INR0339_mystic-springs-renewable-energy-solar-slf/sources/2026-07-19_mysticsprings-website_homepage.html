MYSTIC SPRINGS RENEWABLE ENERGY

Mystic Springs Renewable Energy is a proposed 250-megawatt solar project on approximately 2,500 acres of privately owned land in Kaufman and Hunt Counties, Texas. Alongside its solar capabilities, there is potential for an adjunct 250-megawatt Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). This project could become operational as soon as the end of 2026, offering long-lasting economic benefits to taxpayers, school districts, community colleges, hospitals, and the local government.
‍
If implemented, the storage component would allow Mystic Springs Renewable Energy to store extra solar power generated when demand is low to use when demand for energy increases, which will help provide affordable, reliable energy when it’s needed most.

A STRONGER ECONOMY IN KAUFMAN & HUNT COUNTIES

Mystic Springs Renewable Energy will bring in millions of dollars to the local community over the life of the project. The project will generate a new source of revenue that supports the most essential elements of the community, including schools, the local hospital, community colleges, infrastructure, and local government. With increased local funding and an all-in approach to energy production, Mystic Springs Renewable Energy will help support a reliable, secure Texas grid.

SUPPORT LOCAL LANDOWNERS, WORKERS, AND TAXPAYERS

The Mystic Springs Renewable Energy project will provide new jobs, lease local land, and generate millions of dollars of revenue through taxes and construction spending into the economy. This project will not only support the region financially, but it will also empower local landowners to preserve their family land for future generations.

It will generate American-made, affordable clean energy for residents and businesses. Sustainable energy enables a stronger grid, with secure, reliable energy for everyone. Add your name to the growing list of community members who support the project.
