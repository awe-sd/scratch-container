Wolf Hollow III – Research Summary from Web Sources

Retrieved: 2026-07-19

Source: Yahoo Search aggregated results

Project Basics

  • ERCOT Queue ID: 24INR0567
  • Capacity: 306.32 MW (natural gas-fired)
  • Location: 8787 Wolf Hollow Ct, Granbury, Hood County, TX
  • Zone: ERCOT NORTH
  • Proposed COD: May 30, 2027
  • Technology: Eight new natural gas combustion turbines (CTGs)
  • Status: Pre-construction; TCEQ air permits issued Feb 18, 2025 (after contested case hearing)

Corporate Identity

  • TCEQ Permit Applicant (entity of record): Wolf Hollow II Power, LLC
  • Parent company: Constellation Energy (formerly part of Exelon before 2022 spinoff)
  • Note on Calpine: Constellation acquired Calpine on January 7, 2026 for ~$16.4B equity; Wolf Hollow I & II were Constellation assets pre-acquisition
  • Contact (TCEQ filing): Albert Hatton, Director of Environmental Operations

TCEQ Permit Details

  • Application date: January 25, 2024
  • Permit numbers: 175173 (NSR/PSD TX1636, GHG PSD TX238)
  • TCEQ docket reference: 2024-1918-air
  • Contested Case Hearing: TCEQ granted 180-day CCH on Feb 13, 2025
  • TCEQ final order: February 18, 2025 – permits issued
  • As of Sep 2, 2025: Judge still weighing permit (KERA News)
  • Hood County: Commissioners voted Aug 27, 2024 to urge TCEQ to deny permit

Texas Energy Fund

  • Application: Notice of Intent May 2024; full application July 2024
  • Selected: Yes – for In-ERCOT Generation Loan Program
  • Withdrawn: March 2025 – Constellation withdrew citing permitting delays

POI Substation

  • Mitchell Bend substation – Oncor Electric Delivery, 345kV
  • Part of "Comanche Peak – Wolf Hollow/Mitchell Bend" 345kV line on Oncor system
  • Also connected to Rocky Creek via 345kV overhead line (52.78 km)
  • County location of Mitchell Bend substation: not explicitly confirmed in available sources, but associated with Wolf Hollow/Granbury area

Context

  • Wolf Hollow I: ~720-745 MW CCGT, acquired by Exelon 2011
  • Wolf Hollow II: ~1,115 MW CCGT, began operations 2017
  • Wolf Hollow III: 306 MW addition using 8 new CTGs at same 8787 Wolf Hollow Ct site
  • Site also hosts Bitcoin mining operations (adjacent TotalEnergies facility mentioned)
  • Community opposition in Granbury over air quality; 150+ residents at Sep 2024 hearing
