# T3 Web Sweep — Adamstown Solar (21INR0210)

## Key findings
- Developer LLC: Adams Creek Solar Project, LLC
- Parent developer: Savion LLC (integrated utility-scale solar + storage)
- Official project site: https://www.adamscreeksolarproject.com/
- ~2,618 acres, ~796,000 modules, ~48,400 TX homes powered
- Construction reported started 2026; COD 2027
- PUCT IA link surfaced: https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/Documents/35077_2025_1453242.PDF
- First Amendment to IA filed April 10, 2026
- TX Comptroller Ch.313 agreement with Harrold ISD (#1872), filed 2022
