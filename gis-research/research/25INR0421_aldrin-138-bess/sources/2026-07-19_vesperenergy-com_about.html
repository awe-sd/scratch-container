An Experienced Team of Energy Professionals

Our Company

Vesper Energy provides innovative solutions to today’s complex energy challenges. Headquartered in the greater Dallas area, Vesper Energy is comprised of professionals who have collectively delivered more than 10 GW of renewable energy projects globally. Today, our pipeline includes over 49 renewable energy and storage projects with a generating capacity of 11,000+ MW; enough to power more than 2 million homes. Working with our customers, communities, and business partners, we develop, own, and operate renewable energy projects across the United States with the goal to build a better energy infrastructure.

Our History

Founded in 2015 as Lendlease Energy Development, our team has delivered over 700 MW of renewable energy projects into commercial operation. In 2020, following the acquisition by Magnetar Capital, the company was rebranded as Vesper Energy. Since then, we have grown our pipeline and quadrupled our portfolio of renewable energy and energy storage projects all while expanding our footprint to cover more of the country. Most recently in 2023, GCM Grosvenor joined the company as an equity owner, and their investment will contribute to our continued growth and success.

With our diverse and experienced team, our growing pipeline of renewable energy projects, and the strength of our financial partners and investors, we are prepared and eager to build a clean energy infrastructure.

LEADERSHIP TEAM

Craig Carson

Chairman of the Board of Managers

Craig currently serves as Chairman of the Board of Managers for Vesper Energy. Before assuming his current role, Craig was the CEO of Vesper Energy and founded the business as Lendlease Energy Development in 2014.  Before joining Lendlease, he was the Chief Executive Officer of Infigen Energy US, responsible for all aspects of the firm’s 1GW fleet of wind farms and 600 MW solar development business in the United States. Craig was also the Executive General Manager for Infigen Energy’s global operations and asset management functions. Before that, he served as BP’s US Cogeneration business’s business unit leader and Vice President of projects, operations, and asset management at BP Alternative Energy. He has also held senior management positions with ConocoPhillips and SkyGen Energy. Craig received his Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Illinois at Chicago and a Master’s Degree in Business Administration from Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management.

Mark J. Rostafin

Co-Chief Executive Officer

Mark is a member of the Vesper Energy board of managers and asset management functions at Vesper Energy. Previously at Lendlease Energy Development Mark led origination and finance functions. Prior to joining Lendlease, he held commercial leadership roles at Solaire Generation (acquired by SunPower), NextEra Energy Resources, Smart Energy Capital (acquired by NextEra), Alteris Renewables (acquired by Real Goods Solar) and The Stone House Group. His experience includes strategy development, origination management, and contract negotiation. Mark has experience working with a broad cross-section of counterparties (corporations, government entities, public and investor-owned utilities, and community choice aggregators) and has previously served on the Advisory Board for the Renewable Energy Buyer’s Alliance. Mark received his Bachelor of Arts in Environmental Studies from Gettysburg College and Master Degree of Business Administration – Global Management from the University of Phoenix.

Juan Suarez

Co-Chief Executive Officer

Juan leads all project development activities at Vesper Energy and was previously Vice President of Engineering Procurement and Construction for Vesper Energy and Lendlease Energy Development. Prior to joining the development business, he was responsible for transforming Lendlease’s Distributed Generation Construction Management business into a full engineering, procurement, and construction business. Before joining Lendlease, Juan held various EPC management positions at Gestamp and NRG. Juan was also Vice President at Unirac, a Hilti Group Company. He was a key leader in growing the company from $12M to $100M in revenue in five years. Juan earned a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering from New Mexico State University. He also founded and operated a development and construction company, raising capital, permitting, financing, and constructing residential subdivisions for nine years.

Krish Koomar

Chief Financial Officer

Krish has over two decades of finance and treasury experience in the renewable/conventional power as well in the banking sectors.  He brings significant financing experience having closed transactions exceeding $15 billion (via corporate, project, vendor financings in form of debt and equity as well as M&A transactions) in the U.S and globally.

Krish headed up finance & treasury departments at four major power companies (esVolta, NRG Energy, Edison Mission Energy & InterGen) spanning 20+ years.  He also held energy banker role at WestLB originating and structuring energy transactions.  Early in his career, Krish also had financial structuring roles at Shell Trading & The Williams Companies.

Krish graduated with a distinguished MBA student award for Academic Excellence from the University of South Carolina, Columbia.  He also has a Masters and Undergraduate degree in Commerce from University of Madras, India.

M. Chad Richwine

VP and General Counsel

Chad serves as Vesper Energy’s General Counsel and oversees the Company’s legal and compliance department. Chad has a diverse skillset and background derived from decades of experience working with and for global leaders in private equity, banking, and law.  Chad joined Vesper Energy from Akin Gump Strauss Hauer & Feld LLP where he represented private equity companies, privately and publicly held companies, and ultra-high net worth investors in real estate transactions, mergers and acquisitions, financings (subscription, project, construction, and asset financings), and other transactions and corporate matters.  Before beginning his legal career, Chad spent nearly a decade working for JPMorgan Chase Bank’s Private Bank where he assisted ultra-high net worth clients with investment, credit, and estate planning needs.  While working for JP Morgan, Chad attended Texas A&M University Law School (formerly Texas Wesleyan University School of Law) where he graduated in the top of this class (Magna Cum Laude).

Monica Opderbeck

Chief Commercial Officer

Monica Opderbeck is the Chief Commercial Officer at Vesper Energy, where she oversees Corporate Development & M&A, Origination & Energy Markets, and Commercial Analytics teams with key responsibilities of driving enterprise growth through capital raising, customer engagement for power origination purposes, and analyzing market fundamentals to drive asset optimization and revenue modeling. She brings over 16 years of experience in the energy and renewables space, with a background in principal investing, financing, capital raising, and business development where she has led some of the largest transactions across the U.S. renewable energy market. Prior to Vesper, Monica served as Head of Commercial Origination and Senior Director of Corporate Development at Pattern Energy and spent 12 years at Wells Fargo, primarily as an investor and originator of renewable energy tax equity investments. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics from the University of California, Santa Cruz.
