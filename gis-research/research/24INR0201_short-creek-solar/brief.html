Key evidence

  • Ch313 project boundary (sources/appsupp1_p4.png, Figure 2) georeferenced this pass: scale bar measured at 107 px = 0.4 mi (166.2 px/km); anchor point = boundary's SW corner (pixel ~952,912), matched to the closest point on OSM US-287 way 21326169 to the site longitude, (33.9771, -98.7949), 0.88 km from that way's nearest vertex. Under a north-up affine transform, the boundary's blue outline (detected by color threshold, pixel bbox x:491-1206, y:136-912 excluding the legend) spans lat 33.977-34.019, lon -98.825 to -98.778.
  • The land-disturbance area found via the human-reviewer tip is at lat 34.0475, lon -98.7712 -- 3.1 km north of the boundary's northernmost edge (34.019) and slightly east of its NE corner (-98.778). It falls OUTSIDE the Short Creek Solar / Wichita Solar I Ch313 boundary.
  • The disturbance itself is real (not a false read): imagery/s2_cornercandidate_2022-07-15.png (2022-07-15) shows natural brush/pasture with dark plow-row texture at that location; imagery/s2_cornercandidate_2026-07-15.png (2026-07-15) shows a cleared, notched-boundary graded area with interior dirt tracks, small bright structures, and a small dark pond -- see zoom imagery/s2_cornercandidate_2022vs2026_zoom.png. Cause is unconfirmed from 10 m imagery alone (could be an oil-lease pad/tank battery -- Wichita County is oil country and similar small dot features appear throughout the search grid -- or another unrelated development); it is NOT attributable to 24INR0201.
  • Negative-sweep confirmation: the SW block (site centroid 33.985,-98.795, imagery/key/s2_swblock_2022-07-15.png vs s2_swblock_2026-07-15.png) remains unchanged bare farmland through 2026-07.
  • The reported queue COD (2027-12-17) is INCONSISTENT with the currently-executed contractual COD. The First Amended and Restated IA between AEP Texas and Wichita Solar I, LLC, signed 2026-04-14 (PUCT 35077-2471), sets the Scheduled Commercial Operation Date for Generation Phase 1 (Solar, 24INR0201) at '44 months from the execution date of the Original Agreement' = 44 months from 2024-10-02 = approximately 2028-06-02. The original IA (2024-10-02, 35077-1963) had a Scheduled COD of 50 months = ~2028-12-02; the amendment pulled that in by 6 months while adding a co-located storage phase (27INR0029) with its own COD 56 months out = ~2030-06. The queue-report 2027-12-17 pre-dates BOTH by roughly six months to a year — the queue COD lags the actual contract.
  • Independent imagery evidence, now including a rigorously georeferenced check of the full Ch313 boundary (not just the site centroid), shows the site is completely undeveloped as of 2026-07-15 within the actual project boundary (lat 33.977-34.019, lon -98.825/-98.778). To reach commercial operation on a ~625-830 MW solar-plus-storage plant by Dec 2027, the developer would need to have already completed civil grading, be well into racking, and have the substation transformer pad ready — none of which is visible within the boundary. Reaching Dec 2027 COD from a bare-land starting point in ~17 months is not physically credible for a project of this scale.
  • Reaching the contractual 2028-06 COD is credible given typical build cadence on flat Texas ranchland: mobilization 2027-Q1, grading through 2027-Q4, racking + modules 2028-Q1-Q2, commissioning into In-Service 2028-04 / COD 2028-06.
