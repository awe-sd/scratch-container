## Key facts

- Developer: Intersect Power / IP Meitner, LLC
- Location: East of Pampa, TX, Gray County — Meitner Reinvestment Zone
- Original concept: 400 MW electrolysis (green hydrogen) + 340 MW solar + 460 MW wind
- Revised concept: 840 MW behind-the-meter data center campus
- Phases 1-3: ~600–750k sq ft each; ~40 FTE total
- Phase 1: construction start early 2026, operational late 2028, ~$1B
- Phases 2-3: TBD, ~$1B each
- Both generation projects in-service target: 2027-10-26
- Interconnection: Meitner PV + Meitner Wind → shared 345/34.5 kV substation → excess to ERCOT via Ghost Town Station (new 345 kV switchyard)
- New ~14-mile double-circuit 345 kV line terminating at Gray Substation
- Transmission partner: Cross Texas Transmission
- Tax abatement: 100% property tax relief 10 years/phase; prior hydrogen-focused abatement approved March 2024; vote deferred to Sep 2 meeting
- PUCT/ERCOT filings reference interconnection agreements with Lone Star Infrastructure Protection Act provisions
