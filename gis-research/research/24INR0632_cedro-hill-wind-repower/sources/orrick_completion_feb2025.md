# Source: Orrick — Clearway Completes Repowering of Cedro Hill Wind Farm

URL: https://www.orrick.com/en/News/2025/02/Clearway-Completes-the-Repowering-of-its-Cedro-Hill-Wind-Farm
Retrieved: 2026-07-18

## Key facts
- Completion date: ~February 21, 2025 (article publication)
- Developer: Clearway Energy Group
- Capacity: 160 MW post-repower (from ~150 MW)
- Location: Webb County, Texas
- Investment: $269 million
- Scope: blades + nacelles replaced on 100 turbines
- Power buyer: CPS Energy (100% of output)
- Legal advisor: Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP
- Described as Clearway's "sixth" repower in portfolio, part of "more than 700 MW of upgrades"
- No explicit COD date stated but construction confirmed complete
