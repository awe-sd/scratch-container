  • savevzcounty.org (opposition group, closest thing to a ground observer) still lists Caney Creek as 'Est Build start: Summer 2026' as of 2026-07-20 -- i.e. construction has NOT started per the one non-imagery ground-level source available
  • No PUCT IA amendment, no Ch.313/JETI filing, no EIA-860M listing -- absence of ALL the paper trails that typically accompany active/near-construction projects (compare Hanson Solar dossier, which had 2 IA amendments + Ch313 + EIA all present pre-construction)
  • The only legally binding COD on file with PUCT is the ORIGINAL IA's 2023-06-01 Scheduled Commercial Operation Date (sources/2026-07-19_puct_35077-1353_..., Exhibit B) -- three years stale. No amendment renegotiating this schedule exists anywhere in docket 35077 despite an exhaustive sweep (see contractual_schedule.note) even though the queue's self-reported COD has moved three times since (2023-06 -> 2024-06 -> 2025-05 -> 2027-06, timeline.md)
  • This pattern -- queue COD updates with no corresponding IA amendment on file -- differs from Hanson Solar (23INR0086), where each queue slip was backed by a filed, countersigned Amendment. Here the queue COD looks like a developer/ERCOT-administrative update UNBACKED by a renegotiated interconnection contract, a materially weaker basis for the claim
  • Positive reality signals do exist: $4.28M in real cash security was posted on schedule in 2022 (Exhibit E), and the project remains an active line item in queue reports through 2026-06-01 (not cancelled/inactive)
