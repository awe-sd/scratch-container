The Nolan County Commissioners Court met in regular session Monday to discuss a tax abatement agreement and upgrades to electronic pollbooks.

The court first addressed a proposed tax abatement agreement for CB Sweetwater, LLC, or its subsidiary (collectively “Cloud Burst”), following a presentation by company representatives. Cloud Burst outlined plans for a reinvestment zone featuring improvements exceeding $5.25 million. The project’s initial phase features a next generation, 300-megawatt (MW) AI-ready data center campus.
