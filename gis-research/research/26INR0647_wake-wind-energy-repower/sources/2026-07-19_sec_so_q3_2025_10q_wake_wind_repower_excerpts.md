## KEY EXCERPTS

### 1. Note K — Wind Repowering Projects (Summary)
"During the nine months ended September 30, 2025, Southern Power continued the development project to repower the Kay Wind facility and began development projects to repower the Grant Plains, Grant Wind, and Wake Wind facilities. At September 30, 2025, the total cost of construction incurred related to the projects was $165 million..."

### 2. Schedule table entry (exact text):
"Fourth quarter 2026 Wake Wind Wind 257"
→ Southern Power schedules Wake Wind repower commercial operation: Q4 2026 (257 MW)

### 3. Full capacity description:
"Southern Power continued the development project to repower 200 MWs of the 299-MW Kay Wind facility and began development projects to repower the full capacity of the 147-MW Grant Plains, the 152-MW Grant Wind, and the 257-MW Wake Wind facilities. The output of the development projects is contracted under new and amended PPAs, with commercial operations projected to occur between the third quarter 2026 and the second quarter 2027."

### 4. Construction cost outlook:
"During the second quarter 2025, Southern Power committed to development projects to repower the Grant Plains, Grant Wind, and Wake Wind facilities. At September 30, 2025, the remaining aggregate construction costs for these projects are expected to be between $685 million and $775 million."

### 5. Accelerated depreciation (confirms project is happening, substantial spend):
"Includes accelerated depreciation related to the repowering of the Kay Wind, Grant Plains, Grant Wind, and Wake Wind facilities of $112 million ($80 million after tax, net of noncontrolling interest impacts) and $181 million ($130 million after tax, net of noncontrolling interest impacts), respectively, related to the repowering..."

### 6. Depreciation projection:
"The repowering projects result in accelerated depreciation... projected to total approximately $100 million in 2025, $320 million in 2026, and $25 million in 2027."
→ The $25M in 2027 indicates projects are completing in 2026 with tail into 2027; consistent with COD Q4 2026 for Wake Wind.
