# Web sweep — Alamo City BESS (26INR0330)

## Key findings
- Developer: OCI Energy (ocienergy.com)
- Offtaker: CPS Energy (San Antonio municipal utility, REAP program)
- EPC: Elgin Power Solutions
- Battery supplier: LG Energy Solution Vertech
- Construction financing: ING (closed Sep 2025)
- Capacity: 120 MW / 480 MWh (4-hour duration)
- Location: southeastern Bexar County, TX
- Groundbreaking: May 2026
