## Key facts
- Developer/Owner: AES Corporation
- Location: Taylor and Nolan counties, TX (~23,000 acres)
- Total planned capacity (all phases): 526.5 MW wind
- Replacing 282 older turbines with 117 newer models
- Timeline: Development 2023-25; Decommission/construction/restoration late 2025-2026; Operations 2027
- Status: Under construction
- ~$94M tax revenue over 30 years; 300 peak jobs; 17 long-term ops roles
