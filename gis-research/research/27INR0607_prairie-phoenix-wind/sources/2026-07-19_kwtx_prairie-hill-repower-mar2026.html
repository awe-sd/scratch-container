Prairie Hill wind farm replacement project continues near Mart

By Nate Smith
Published: Mar. 31, 2026 at 4:24 PM CDT
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

MART, Texas (KWTX) - Crews are continuing to take down wind turbines at the Prairie Hill Wind Farm near Mart as part of a project to replace the entire farm.

ENGIE, the energy company behind the wind farm, said in February it planned to take down all 100 wind turbines at Prairie Hill Farm and replace them with 63 models. While this cuts the number of turbines in use in half, the company said the new set will still produce roughly the same amount of power, 300 megawatts.

The company said teardown is well underway.

“Work is proceeding well at Prairie Hill. We’ve been able to fell about 20% of the wind turbines to date, and more expected to come in the month of April,” said Julie Vitek, vice president of public affairs for ENGIE North America.

The work includes a process called “felling,” where crews bring down multiple turbines a day using brief explosions. After that, each turbine is taken apart and shipped off for recycling.

“Steel has already been making its way to Houston, and fiberglass and the other blade material has been making its way to Amarillo for recycling,” Vitek said. “And later in April, crews will begin dismantling the concrete foundations. That concrete will be shipped to Waco for recycling.”

Vitek said there could be potential road impacts as the parts are taken out of town.

“As crews begin taking materials off-site, there could be some temporary traffic road closures. But we will work to minimize those,” she said. “This work is all taking place during daylight hours, as well.”

ENGIE said its original schedule is holding, but weather could cause delays.

“The summer can bring some unpredictable weather, and this work is weather dependent,” Vitek said. “We’d be concerned about doing the work in torrential rain, as well as high winds.”

The decommissioning process is expected to take until late August or early September. After that, the installation of new turbines will take about a year.
