# Source: Deriva Energy — Maryneal Windpower Project

URL: https://derivaenergy.com/projects/maryneal-windpower-project/
Fetched: 2026-07-18

## Key facts
- Developer: Deriva Energy (formerly Duke Energy Sustainable Solutions)
- Capacity: 182 MW
- Location: Nolan County (Maryneal), Texas
- Commercial Operation Date: Not stated on page (external sources say July 2021)
- Offtaker/PPA: Sprint (now T-Mobile) — 12-year virtual PPA (VPPA); ~30% of Sprint's total electricity portfolio
- Turbines: 38 × Nordex Acciona 4.8 MW
- EPC Contractor: Wanzek Construction
- Energy impact: ~54,000 homes; emission reduction ~96,000 vehicles

## Status
Multiple secondary sources (ercotqueue.com, cleanview.co, power-technology.com) confirm
project COMMISSIONED ~July 2021. ERCOT queue still shows this INR as active with
approvedForSynchronization = 2021-04-23 but no approvedForCommercialOperation date.
