Parliament Solar Case Study
Parliament Solar, our namesake project, is a 640MWdc utility-scale solar project located just outside Houston in Waller County, Texas. Completed in early 2025, the project reflects Parliament’s decades of experience developing, building, and managing stable, high-performing energy assets, executed with a disciplined, safety-first approach focused on long-term reliability. Now fully operational, Parliament Solar generates enough clean energy to power approximately 88,000 homes a year, contributing meaningful capacity to the Texas grid while supporting regional energy resilience through responsible, long-term stewardship.

Procurement standards & practices
Parliament Energy is dedicated to transparency, fairness, and sustainability in procurement. We work with suppliers who share our commitment to quality and ethical conduct, regardless of location. Through a rigorous and consistent evaluation process, we build long-term, responsible partnerships that support reliable project delivery and strong community relationships.
Sustainability of material used
We ensure the quality and durability of our materials by selecting high-performance resources that meet our technical and operational standards. We focus on components that support long asset life and reliable project performance. Our supply-chain practices emphasize consistency, accountability, and responsible sourcing across every stage of development and operations.
safety philosophy
We are committed to mitigating safety risk through clear communication, active engagement, and a culture of empowerment and accountability. All technicians complete safety-orientation training and are required to use appropriate personal protective equipment every day. Job-specific certifications and procedures are outlined in the safety manual and reinforced by management. Safety stand-downs occur at the start and end of each workday and before new tasks to ensure alignment and hazard awareness. Safety is—and remains—our highest operational priority.
