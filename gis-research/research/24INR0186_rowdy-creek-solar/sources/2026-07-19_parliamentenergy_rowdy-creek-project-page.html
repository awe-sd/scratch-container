about the project

Rowdy Creek is a large-scale solar development in Lamar County designed to support Texas’ growing energy demand while investing in the strength of local communities. Built with a focus on long-term performance and responsible land use, the project brings together skilled labor, durable infrastructure, and thoughtful planning. Once operational, Rowdy Creek will contribute to a more reliable grid and deliver lasting economic and environmental benefits to the region.
