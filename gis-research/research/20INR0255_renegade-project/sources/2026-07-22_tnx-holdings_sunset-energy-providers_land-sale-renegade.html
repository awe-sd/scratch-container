    Sunset Energy Providers, LLC

    Location: Deaf Smith, TX
    Land Area: ~1,310 acres
    Status: sold

    TNX Holdings acted as senior secured lender to Sunset Energy Providers, LLC ("Sunset"), which previously owned 1,310 acres of land in Deaf Smith County — in the Texas Panhandle. In October 2025, Sunset has successfully sold the land and TNX Holdings is no longer acting as lender.

    This land is expected to ultimately support the Renegade Solar project. Purchased by Sunset in 2022, the approximately 1,310 acres will host this NTP-stage, utility-scale solar photovoltaic farm. The project substation has already been constructed on the site.


    Interconnection Switch

    The Renegade Solar project is designed to deliver a clean, renewable energy capacity of around 515 MW_ac, enough to power more than 100,000 homes. As of mid‑2025, development financing and construction are actively advancing. While TNX has exited ownership interests in the Renegade project itself, we are pleased to support Sunset Energy Provider's land ownership interests.
