KEY EXCERPT:

"During the third quarter of 2025, AES Renewable Holdings completed buyouts of tax equity
partners at Buffalo Gap I, Buffalo Gap II, and Buffalo Gap III, resulting in a decrease to
NCI of $28 million and a decrease to additional paid-in capital of $42 million."

SIGNIFICANCE:
- AES consolidated 100% ownership of all three Buffalo Gap wind farms in Q3 2025 (Jul–Sep 2025)
- Tax equity buyout is a standard pre-repower step — existing tax equity must be cleared
  before the repowering project can use new tax credits (ITC/PTC)
- Clears the capital structure for the repower; HASI preferred equity followed in Dec 2025
