# Source: Mockingbird Solar Center Commissioning (Electrek, Nov 22 2024)

URL: https://electrek.co/2024/11/22/orsted-largest-solar-farm-in-the-world-is-now-online-and-in-texas/

## Key facts
- Project: Mockingbird Solar Center (= Baker Branch Solar / 23INR0026)
- Developer: Ørsted
- COD: ~November 22, 2024 (article published on commissioning day)
- Capacity: 468 MW
- Location: Near Roxton and Brookston, Lamar County, Texas
- First Solar supplied panels; Drake Construction + Pfifer Farms as local contractors
- 550+ construction jobs; ~$75M in local property taxes
- Ørsted donated 953 acres adjacent native prairie to The Nature Conservancy (Smiley Meadow Preserve)
- Ørsted's largest solar project globally at time of commissioning

## Relevance
Confirms the project is ALREADY OPERATIONAL as of Nov 2024. Reported COD of 2026-09-30 in the ERCOT queue is stale/not yet updated in the queue to reflect actual COD.
