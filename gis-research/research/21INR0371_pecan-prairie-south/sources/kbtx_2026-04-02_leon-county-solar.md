# KBTX: Large-Scale Solar Farm Transforms Rural Leon County Landscape
Source: kbtx.com/2026/04/02/large-scale-solar-farm-transforms-rural-leon-county-landscape/
Fetched: 2026-07-18

## Key facts
- Project: Pecan Prairie Solar Facility (595 MW total; Pecan Prairie South = ~133 MW portion)
- Developer/Owner: **Repsol** (acquired ConnectGen in 2024; ConnectGen was original developer)
- Location: Leon County, TX — near **Marquez** (adjacent to Streater family land)
- Land: ~1,300 acres; formerly farm/ranch land
- Groundbreaking: **October 2025** — construction actively underway as of April 2026
- Expected operational: **2027**
- Transmission lines run from Limestone County through Leon County into Grimes County
- Community opposition group: Citizens for Responsible Growth in Leon County (crglc.org)
